## How to read the coverage

- **Same argument**: the mathematical argument printed in the paper, allowing routine changes of variables, explicit integrability lemmas, or equivalent finite-sum notation.
- **Alternative bypass**: the result is proved, but a substantive printed step is bypassed.
- **Unverified step**: the printed step still lacks a checked counterpart, awaits a formal composition bridge, or needs a stated repair.

A checked intermediate theorem can verify a printed transition even when an older terminal theorem retains an alternative proof value. This report distinguishes such compositional coverage from an endpoint whose proof value itself follows the added route. In particular, the new `sector_exists_logistic_matrix_paper_constant` endpoint propagates the full-sector B5 input through B4 and applies Fatou to the full padded matrix before compression; the older matrix endpoint remains available.
